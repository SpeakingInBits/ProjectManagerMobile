## Installing

Download the file for your phone from **Assets** below. Everything stays on your phone; the app has no accounts and no network access.

### Android

Download `ProjectManager-android.apk` on your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps, then tap **Install**. Android 8.0 or later.

To update, install the new version's APK over the old one. Your data stays.

`ProjectManager-android.aab` is the app bundle for uploading to Google Play. You don't need it to install the app.

### iOS

`ProjectManager-ios.ipa` installs on the iPhones and iPads its provisioning profile includes. Connect the device to a Mac, open it in Finder, and drag the `.ipa` onto it (or use Apple Configurator). If the profile is an App Store one, upload the `.ipa` to App Store Connect with the Transporter app and install it from TestFlight instead. iOS 15 or later.
